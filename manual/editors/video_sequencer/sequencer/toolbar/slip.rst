.. _tool-slip:

*********
Slip Tool
*********

.. reference::

   :Editor:    Video Sequencer
   :Tool:      :menuselection:`Toolbar --> Slip`

The Slip tool moves only the content of a strip without changing its position or duration.
By default, movement is limited by the content available beyond the strip handles.
When this limit is reached, the strip outline is drawn in red.

Press :kbd:`C` to toggle this limit.
